## E5 Decision, uncertainty, deliverables
Coverage engineering gates: 100% date/symbol/label invariants, reproducibility; report all denominators; market B coverage >=75%/80% unchanged, full calendar verified; at least 80% of planned feature-ready eligible core observations fully scored in each split and >=80% label completion among scored observations, also show all-draw eligibility (no threshold bypass); if under gate analyze attrition and do not generalize. Report at least 100 unique evaluated entry episodes and >=30 per regime for regime claims (descriptive minimum, not power guarantee), >=20 distinct date blocks for primary inference, else inconclusive. Cluster uncertainty using date moving blocks length 40 exchange sessions (weekly equivalent >=8 weeks), keep cross-section together; stock-cluster sensitivity; 95% intervals, report effective dates not raw row n. For confirmatory directional improvement primary paired equal-date rank IC and bullish-bearish spread, require both consistent sign across validation/holdout and no material downside degradation (MAE tail/worst quantile); 95% block interval for primary improvement above zero after multiplicity, otherwise retain baseline/inconclusive. Strategy candidate only if positive cost-stressed net advantage vs baseline matched execution and max drawdown/CVaR not materially worse (proposed max drawdown worsening <=2 percentage points, CVaR5% worsening <=0.5pp), adequate trades, robustness across regimes, no leak; thresholds require approval and are not guarantees. Report absolute levels, not just improvements. Do not tune risk guards to increase signal count. No change unless independent later user approval. If all arms sparse or fail, recommend retain baseline or redesign with new untouched data. Real prospective validation after historical holdout required before live.
Deliver: E0 coverage/entitlement/runtime report and manifest; E1 frozen sample manifest and per-split attrition; E2 baseline evidence/event and signal funnel report; E3 single-factor comparison and selected challengers locked; E4 chronological out-of-sample execution results only if data suffice; E5 decision memo/negative results, reproducibility commit/hash and full limitations.
